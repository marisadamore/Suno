
# Starbucks Spring Jingle – AI Commercial Music Project

This repository documents an AI-assisted music workflow focused on developing a short-form commercial jingle inspired by Starbucks in the spring season.  

The project demonstrates how generative AI tools can be used to move from creative brief to finished audio asset, with a focus on concise brand messaging suitable for advertising.

---

## 1. Creative Brief & Song Concept (ChatGPT)

**Prompt Used:**

> "Create a short, catchy 20–30 second commercial jingle inspired by Starbucks in the spring. The lyrics should be concise, memorable, and brand-forward, suitable for a radio or social media advertisement. Emphasize sunny mornings, fresh coffee, energy, and togetherness. Keep the structure simple with a strong hook and repeatable tagline. The tone should be upbeat, modern, and feel-good."

---

## 2. Jingle Lyrics (Generated by ChatGPT)

**Hook / Chorus**  
Spring is calling, feel the glow,  
Fresh brewed mornings, on the go.  
Sip the sunshine, take it slow,  
Starbucks starts your day just right.

**Tagline Repeat**  
Bright days begin with Starbucks.

*(Approximate runtime: 20–25 seconds when produced with repeat hook.)*

---

## 3. Suno Style Prompt (Generated by ChatGPT)

> "Generate a 20–30 second upbeat commercial jingle inspired by Starbucks in the spring. The mood should be bright, energetic, and optimistic. Use light acoustic guitar, rhythmic claps, soft bass, and clean pop percussion. Tempo should be lively (110–120 BPM). Structure: Intro hit – Hook – Tagline repeat – Quick instrumental outro. The melody should be catchy, simple, and easy to remember, suitable for advertising and social media use."
