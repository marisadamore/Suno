# Suno# Starbucks in Spring – AI Song Project

This repository demonstrates my AI-assisted music project, creating an uplifting acoustic pop song inspired by Starbucks in spring. The project showcases the workflow from **song idea generation**, **lyrics creation**, to **music style prompt for Suno**, highlighting my work with AI tools.

---

## 1. Song Idea & Prompt (ChatGPT)

**Prompt generated for song idea:**

> "Create an uplifting acoustic pop song celebrating Starbucks in the spring. The lyrics should evoke the warmth of sunny mornings, fresh coffee aromas, blooming flowers, and a sense of community. Include a catchy chorus about enjoying life’s small moments with a cup of coffee. The mood should be bright, cheerful, and inspiring, with a moderate tempo suitable for a feel-good commercial."

## 3. Suno Style Prompt (Generated by ChatGPT)

> "Generate an uplifting acoustic pop song inspired by Starbucks in spring. The mood should be bright, cheerful, and warm. Use acoustic guitar, light piano, and soft drums as the primary instruments, with gentle strings in the background to create a sense of blooming and freshness. Include a moderate tempo (around 100–110 BPM) and a catchy, singable melody for the vocals. The lyrics should evoke sunny mornings, the aroma of coffee, friends gathering, blooming flowers, and simple joyful moments. Build the song structure as Verse – Pre-Chorus – Chorus – Verse – Pre-Chorus – Chorus – Bridge – Chorus/Outro."
